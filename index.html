On-Set Studio

A virtual soundstage that runs inside ComfyUI. Free.

Stage rigged characters, light them, place and move the camera, then hand the model the scene you actually built instead of a description of it.

  • Pixel-aligned depth, normal, canny, and OpenPose passes, mattes, beauty renders, and scene JSON, as stills or sequences
  • Camera rail and shot system with a multi-camera View Finder
  • Lighting rigs with 19 portrait presets and physical bounce
  • Gaussian splat sets with character occlusion, reference-plate camera matching, and text-to-motion animation through NVIDIA's ARDY
